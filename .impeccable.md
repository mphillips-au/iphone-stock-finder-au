## Design Context
### Users
One Australian iPhone buyer checking launch-day retail availability, often on mobile while travelling. Find an exact configuration and inspect a store's entire tracked inventory.
### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, premium. An original unofficial utility, with no implication of Telstra or Apple affiliation.
### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial restraint, generous whitespace, strong left-aligned type, near-white and charcoal, electric blue actions. Compact rows rather than a dashboard card grid. Green means confirmed stock only.
### Design Principles
1. Actual inventory and freshness take priority over decoration.
2. Search is immediate; secondary detail is progressively disclosed.
3. Mobile actions are reachable, readable and at least 44px tall.
4. Errors and unknown inventory are truthful and recoverable.
5. Keyboard operation, focus, contrast and reduced motion are built in.
